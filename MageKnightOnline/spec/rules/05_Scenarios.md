# Scenarios and Variants

## Game Modes
* **Standard**: A mix of exploration, conquest, and character building.
* [cite_start]**Cooperative**: Players work together to assault cities or survive[cite: 11579].
* [cite_start]**Competitive**: "Last man standing" or Fame races[cite: 11392].

## [cite_start]Scenario Book [cite: 11713]
The Scenario Book contains specific rules for:
1.  **Map Shape**: Wedge, Rectangular, or Open.
2.  **City Levels**: Determining the difficulty of defenders.
3.  **End Conditions**: Round limits or specific achievements (e.g., Conquering all Cities).

## [cite_start]Cooperative City Assault [cite: 10839]
* Multiple players can assault a city together.
* **Combat**: Players agree on how to split enemies and block assignments.
* **Scoring**: Points are shared or calculated based on contribution.

## Scoring
* **Fame**: The primary metric for winning. Gained by defeating enemies, conquering sites, and exploring.
* **Reputation**: Affects interaction with locals (Influence costs). High reputation makes recruiting easier; low reputation (from burning monasteries or pillaging) makes it harder.
