# Game Structure

## Rounds
The game is played over several **Rounds**, alternating between **Day** and **Night**.
* **Round End**: A round ends when a player's Deed deck is empty and they announce the end of the round.
* **Day/Night Board**: Tracks the current time. [cite_start]Day and Night affect movement costs and mana die usage[cite: 11750].

## Turn Structure
On a player's turn, they generally perform the following:
1.  **Preparation**: Refresh Unit offers or specialized displays if required.
2.  **Action**:
    * [cite_start]**Regular Turn**: Play cards to Move, Interact, or Fight[cite: 10049].
    * [cite_start]**Resting**: Discard a non-wound card to heal or refresh hand[cite: 10083].
3.  **End of Turn**:
    * Discard played cards to the discard pile.
    * [cite_start]Draw back up to your Hand Limit (determined by your Level token)[cite: 11778].
    * If your Deed deck is empty, you may announce the "End of the Round".

## Player Order
* [cite_start]Determined by the **Round Order tokens** selected at the start of each round (Tactics selection)[cite: 11722].
* The player who picks the earliest tactic goes first.

## Day vs. Night
* **Vision**: Exploring map tiles costs more movement at night (unless using specific skills/spells).
* **Mana**: Gold mana dice in the Source cannot be used at Night (Black mana is used instead).
