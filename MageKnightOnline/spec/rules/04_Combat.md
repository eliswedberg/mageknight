# Combat Rules

## Combat Sequence
Combat follows a strict phase order. Players must resolve each phase fully before moving to the next.

### [cite_start]1. Ranged and Siege Attack Phase [cite: 10804]
* Players may use **Ranged** or **Siege** attacks to eliminate enemies before they strike.
* **Resistances**: Enemies may be resistant to Physical, Fire, or Ice attacks.

### 2. Block Phase
* **Enemy Attack**: If the enemy was not eliminated, they attack.
* **Blocking**: Players generate **Block** points using cards or Unit abilities.
* **Elements**: Fire attacks require Ice/Cold Fire block (or efficient physical block); Ice attacks require Fire/Cold Fire block.
* **Multiple Attacks**: Each attack must be blocked separately.

### 3. Assign Damage Phase
* If an attack is unblocked (or partially blocked), the Hero takes damage.
* **Wounds**: Damage is converted into Wounds.
    * [cite_start]**Wounding Units**: You may assign damage to Units, wounding them[cite: 11051].
    * **Wounding Hero**: Remaining damage adds Wound cards to your hand.
* **Armor**: Your Armor value (on Level token) determines how much damage absorbs one Wound.

### [cite_start]4. Attack Phase [cite: 10743]
* If the enemy is still alive, the Hero makes a standard attack.
* **Melee**: Standard attacks are used here.
* **Defeating**: Damage must equal or exceed the enemy's Armor/Health.

## Special Abilities
* [cite_start]**Red Mana**: Effects powered by Red Mana often disable non-Attack/Block effects of enemies[cite: 10695].
* **Vampiric**: Increases enemy armor based on wounds dealt.
* **Cumbersome**: Allows players to reduce enemy attack value by spending Move points.

## Recovery
* **Healing**: Damage/Wounds can be healed using Healing points. [cite_start]Unspent healing disappears when entering combat[cite: 10954].
* [cite_start]**Timing**: You can heal immediately after combat on the same turn[cite: 10893].
